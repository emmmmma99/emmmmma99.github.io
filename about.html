About Me

Hellooo

My name is Emma, and I originally created this website as a personal project to improve my HTML and CSS skills. A few years later, I thought it made sense to give it a new life and turn it into a portfolio where I can share some of the projects I’ve worked on along the way.

After almost three years living in Paris, and some time volunteering and backpacking in Hawaii, I recently moved back to Barcelona to continue developing my career in event production and operations.

During my time in Paris, I worked across event production, operations and project coordination. I’m curious, reliable and detail-oriented, and I enjoy working on projects with lots of different people.

I’m particularly interested in projects connected to culture, fashion, media, music and live events, as well as urban life and design.

This portfolio is a collection of some of the projects I’ve worked on so far, along with a few other things I’m interested in.
